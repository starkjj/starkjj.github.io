How it works

Click points on the ground to lay out the line. Each corner point becomes a curve: a transition (easement) spiral, a circular arc at the point's radius, and a spiral back out. The track runs tangent to the dashed lines between points.

Height is the underside of the sleepers above the baseboard. Gradients run between the middles of the curves and are smoothed with vertical curves. Drag points up or down in the profile strip at the bottom.

Radii follow the Gauge O Guild guidance: about 1200 mm is the practical minimum for mainline stock (with gauge widening), and 1500 mm is the comfortable minimum. Below 1600 mm the gauge is widened by up to 0.4 mm (max 32.4 mm).

Generate pieces splits the line into pieces that fit your bed, turning them diagonally when that fits more track. Each piece has a dovetail socket at its start and a tab at its end, copied from your TrackDouble.blend joint. The export also includes risers to hold the track at the right height, a 1:1 SVG plan, and a README.
